# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep feedback about the work and not the person, and do not harass anyone. Report problems privately through GitHub's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form or by contacting the maintainers through the Wheewise website; reports are handled in confidence.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules.
